# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

During this laboratory mission, I gained a better understanding of the three major cloud computing platforms: **Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. Among the three, **GCP impressed me the most** because of its strong capabilities in **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Kubernetes, data management, and application development**. I also learned that AWS, Azure, and GCP offer many similar services, including **virtual machines, cloud storage, databases, networking, security, and container services**. All three platforms also use a **pay-as-you-go pricing model**, allowing organizations to pay based on the resources they use.

I learned that choosing the right cloud platform depends on several factors, such as an organization's **budget, business requirements, security, performance, scalability, existing technologies, and future goals**. For example, startups may prefer flexible and affordable services, while larger organizations may prioritize reliability, scalability, and global availability. Businesses that already use Microsoft products may also benefit more from choosing Azure.

Working as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me that no single cloud provider is the best choice for every organization. It is important to understand the client's needs, compare the available services, and recommend a platform that matches their requirements. This activity also helped improve my **research, decision-making, problem-solving, and communication skills**.

Completing this mission also made my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** more organized and informative. I added research findings, comparison tables, decision matrices, Linux investigation results, and client recommendations. Overall, this laboratory strengthened my knowledge of cloud computing and helped me develop a more practical understanding of how cloud platforms can be **evaluated, compared, selected, and recommended based on real-world business requirements**.
